## 2. Source rule: notes are a paraphrase

### What you asked
"Short pick" and "level of detail" were filled from your meeting notes. Notes are your paraphrase, so they should stay assumed until the stakeholder confirms. Show which source confirmed each one, and fix and test it if notes alone confirmed anything.

### What the run before the fix showed (`70cd651`)

| Source | "Level of detail" | "Short pick" |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Sam's email reply | No quote the code could verify in the source, so not recorded | Found; definition hedged ("not sure…"), kept as a draft |
| 2. **Your notes** | **covered**, from your notes alone | **confirmed**, from your notes alone |
| 3. Follow-up transcript | (no change) | (no change) |

So yes: notes alone confirmed both. That broke the rule.

### The fix (`c3f826e`)
- **Answers.** One that rests only on notes is stored as **assumed**, never covered. If a stakeholder source already covered the area, the notes add their quote and change nothing else.
- **Definitions.** One from notes is stored as a **draft**. The term stays unconfirmed and keeps blocking Go ("no confirmed definition, no Go"). If the owner has already confirmed a definition, notes can only **propose** a different one. They never replace it.
- **Disagreements.** A disagreement settled by notes stays assumed.
- **Corrections.** Your corrections through `correct` are recorded as notes, so the same rule applies: a corrected area is assumed, and a corrected definition is a draft.
- **Follow-up.** The follow-up packet asks the stakeholder to confirm each notes-only item, required **or optional**:
  - Area: *"Our notes say: '…'. Is that right? Please say it in your own words."*
  - Term: *"Our notes define 'short pick' as '…'. Is that right? Please say what counts and what doesn't, in your own words."*
- **Readiness lines.** These say "from notes only, needs the stakeholder to confirm".
- **Spec.** The HANDOFF.md decision "notes count as answered" is replaced by the source rule.

### Tests added (`tests/ingest.test.ts`, "source rule…")
1. An answer from notes alone stays assumed, and the follow-up asks the stakeholder to confirm it.
2. A definition from notes alone is a draft. The term stays unconfirmed and blocks Go.
3. The stakeholder's own words then confirm both, and the follow-up questions go away.
4. Notes never change a definition the owner confirmed; a different one is only proposed.

The first draft of test 1 also exposed a real gap: the gap finder only checked required areas, so a notes-only *optional* area was never sent for confirmation. That's fixed in the same commit.

### After the fix: which source backs each answer (`b2d6672`)

| Area / term | Status | Backed by |
|---|---|---|
| core.users | covered | reply (notes agreed) |
| core.outcome | covered | reply |
| core.data | covered | reply |
| core.systems | covered | reply |
| core.constraints | assumed | reply (partial answer) |
| core.trust | covered | reply (its source wasn't being recorded; fixed in `1cf366b`) |
| data_report.decision | covered | reply |
| data_report.definition | assumed | reply (hedged) plus notes |
| data_report.grain (level of detail) | covered | **reply** this run; notes agreed |

## 3. Existing asset: partial vs covers

### What you asked
Confirm that "the dashboard covers part of the need" is recorded as **partial**, not covers, and can't force Don't build.

### Findings

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Did the existing-asset **check** see it as partial coverage? | Yes: `existing_asset: yes` (after the prompt fix in `dc0133e`; it said "no" before). |
| What **band** did the write-up record? | **`none`**, which is wrong. It should be `partial`. |
| Can it force Don't build? | **No.** Only `covers` can force Don't build (`scoring.yaml`, `dont_build_definitive.existing_asset: [covers]`). In async, even `covers` counts only if you confirm that reason at sign-off by its key (`item-1:existing_asset`); unconfirmed, it routes to Gather more. |
| Is there a test? | Yes: `gates.test.ts`, "a partial existing asset doesn't stop the build" (partial still reaches Go). The async CLI test covers an unconfirmed `covers` going to Gather more. |

### Proposed fix (not built)
Code reconciles the check with the band:
- check **yes** means at least `partial`, and `covers` only when the write-up says covers.
- check **no** means `none`.
- check unclear or never asked means the write-up's band stands.

This also needs a test with the smoke-test shape: check yes, write-up `none`, so the band becomes `partial`.

## 6. Regression: persona 7, async

Command: `scripts/eval-async.mjs --personas enablement-kit --formats email,notes`. Commit `3e7de4b`. Kit on gpt-4.1, simulated requester on gpt-4.1-mini. $0.16.

Triage (type and modifiers) comes from the persona card, because it's your call in async mode. Routing isn't measured here.

| Format | Gates | Expected | Match | First answer filled | Follow-ups | Open at sign-off | Invented quotes | Quotes dropped |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| email | known_idea **Go**; manual_process Don't build (superseded) | Gather more | **no** | 13/13 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| notes | known_idea **Gather more** | Gather more | yes | 0/13 (all assumed, by the source rule) | 2 | 17 | 0 | 0 |

### Email miss: the simulated requester invented facts
Persona 7's situation has **no numbers and no manual process**. The simulated requester's email nonetheless said:
- "rollout plans may take several days to prepare… A target would be to reduce planning time to under an hour"
- "use-case ranking and rollout planning are often done by hand in spreadsheets"

Given those words, the kit behaved correctly:
- `success_metric` was rated `measurable_with_baseline`, which made it Go.
- The manual-work "yes" spawned an item.
- That item was superseded by item 1, and the requester confirmed the superseded reason.

The likely cause is the packet's example answers. They contain numbers ("about four hours every Monday"), which may prompt a model, or a real person, to supply one.

**Two separate takeaways:**
1. **Eval harness:** tighten the simulated requester's prompt so it never invents numbers or processes beyond the persona's situation, then rerun.
2. **Product:** the packet examples might lead real respondents. Options:
   - keep the examples, but say "only if you have a real number"
   - use examples without numbers for the outcome question

### Notes run: correct, but it shows two issues
- **Everything stayed assumed.** The source rule worked as designed: an intake fed only notes can't confirm anything, even after two follow-ups answered in notes. That's the rule; in real use the follow-up would go to the stakeholder.
- **Term-finding over-flags (new issue).** Ingest flagged "adoption report", "risk-card", "intake tool spec packs" and "use-case ranker script" as business terms. These are the requester's own deliverables, not terms with a contested meaning. Each one becomes a Gather more reason and an open question. The prompt needs a tighter definition of a term: a word whose meaning varies by business or person (on-time, fill rate, short pick), not the name of something being built.

### Cosmetic issue (new)
Item titles in async come from the request text, truncated to 80 characters. A spawned item's title comes from the model's value, also truncated. So the confirm question at sign-off read: *"'Use-case ranking and rollout planning are often done by hand in spreadsheets or ' would go away once 'I want hiring managers to see I can do the enablement side of AI, not just the b' exists…"*. Titles should be short summaries.
